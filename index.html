Privacy Policy

This policy explains how SONA — Smart Store Management handles your information when you use the app, including its optional Google Drive backup feature.

Last updated: October 14, 2026 Developer: Drogon Dev Studio
In short: Store Manager is designed to work offline. Business records are stored on your device. Google Drive backup is optional and uses your Google account only when you choose to connect it.

1. Overview

SONA — Smart Store Management (“Store Manager,” “the app,” “we,” or “our”) is an offline-first business tracking application designed to help merchants record sales, manage inventory, and generate receipts.

2. Data Storage and Ownership

  • Offline storage: Business records, including store profiles, inventory items, prices, sales history, customer contacts, and receipts, are stored locally on your device in the app’s SQLite database.
  • No Drogon Dev Studio servers: Drogon Dev Studio does not operate cloud database servers, analytics collectors, or advertising trackers for this app. We do not receive or sell your business records through our own servers.

3. Optional Google Account and Google Drive Backup

  • Optional connection: Connecting a Google Account is optional. Store Manager is designed to remain usable without a Google account or an internet connection for its offline features.
  • Backup transfer: If you enable Google Drive Backup, the app uses Google OAuth to authenticate you and transfers backup data to your own Google Drive account.
  • Application data folder: Backups are stored in Google Drive’s private, app-specific appDataFolder. The app requests the drive.appdata scope to access its own backup snapshots in that folder.
  • Personal Drive files: The app is not designed to access your personal files, photos, or documents stored elsewhere in Google Drive.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • You can stop automatic backups by setting the backup frequency to “Never,” or stop using the connected account by selecting “Disconnect Google Account” in Settings.
  • Disconnecting your Google Account does not delete the local database on your device.
  • You can delete cloud backups through the app’s restore/backup interface, if the app provides that option, or manage the app’s stored data through your Google Account settings.
